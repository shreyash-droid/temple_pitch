Team projectReinforcement learning

Bipedal locomotion, sim → real

We're training a walking policy in NVIDIA Isaac Sim for a custom 6-DOF humanoid leg designed in CAD, with DC motors, an ESP32, motor drivers and an MPU6050 IMU. PPO maps joint angles, velocities and IMU orientation to motor torque, with domain randomisation and a manipulability reward term to narrow the sim-to-real gap.
