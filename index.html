Academic group projectWearable

NeuroCardio: a heart-monitoring wearable

An Arduino prototype with an analog ECG sensor and an electrode belt. I replaced the fake data we inherited with about 109k real heartbeats from the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, then compared three kinds of model to see which spots irregular beats best.

Team projectReinforcement learning

A robotic leg that learns to walk

We're teaching a robotic leg to walk with reinforcement learning. We designed the leg in CAD (6 joints, DC motors, an ESP32 controller and a motion sensor) and train it in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. The model reads joint angles and body tilt and decides how hard each motor pushes. We deliberately vary the simulation so what works in sim still works on real hardware.
